# Login

To log in to **OpenVLE**, please open the individual portal URL of your installation.  
You typically receive this URL from your **IT department**, your **organization**, or via a corresponding **invitation email**.

If you are not yet logged in, you will be automatically redirected to the login page.

---

## Login with an External Account

Users with an **external account** log in with their usual credentials:

- **Username:** your central username
- **Password:** your personal password

These credentials are managed externally.  
If you experience login issues, please contact your IT department or technical support.

---

## Login with a Local Account

If you do not have an external account (e.g., external persons or test users), you can log in with a **local account**.  
This consists of a separate **username** and **password** that were individually assigned to you.  
Local accounts are managed exclusively within **OpenVLE**.

---

## Example or Use Case

<Example title="Login via the login page">
  After opening your individual OpenVLE portal URL, the login screen appears.
  Enter your **username** and **password** and click **Login**. Upon successful
  authentication, you are automatically taken to the **Dashboard**.
</Example>

## Notes / Special Considerations

- Passwords are transmitted and stored securely encrypted.
- After multiple incorrect attempts, access may be temporarily locked.
- Local user accounts are independent of the central login system.
- After logging in, your session remains active until you manually log out or close the browser.
- For security reasons, it is recommended to manually log out after use — especially on public devices.
